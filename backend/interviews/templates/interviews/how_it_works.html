Built-in safeguards

  • No double bookingRow lock on the slot + a partial unique index on live bookings. The second student gets “That time was just taken”.
  • Interviewer can't make itBooking moves to a free colleague at the same time; otherwise cancelled with priority rebooking, no attempt used.
  • Call drops / no-showTechnical issue uses no attempt. No-show only after 10 min; the 2nd uses an attempt.
  • Malpractice after the badgeFlag → on hold → confirm nullifies the badge, reverses Miles, re-ranks the leaderboard.
  • DeadlineNo slots past the deadline; cooldowns that cross it are flagged to ops.
  • AuditEvery state change is written to the audit log with actor and reason.
